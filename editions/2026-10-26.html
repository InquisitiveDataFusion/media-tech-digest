United Kingdom
Regulation & policy

Media figures strongly criticised proposals to close linear services for BBC Three, BBC Scotland and Radio 4 Extra. Television executives also attacked potential UK government plans for a YouTube tax to fund the public broadcaster.

BBC News will eliminate 100 jobs to save five point five million pounds. The cuts include losing dedicated education and health editor positions, raising concerns among educational leaders about future sector scrutiny.

Regulator Ofcom admitted that UK online safety laws have failed to reduce social media harm among children during their first nine months. Separately, a US judge ordered Donald Trump to sit for a deposition by 11 December in his defamation lawsuit against the BBC.

Audience & ratings

Commercial broadcaster ITV commissioned a sports focused digital series titled The Sidestep. The show is produced by BE Studio and stars British rugby players Marcus Smith and Louis Rees-Zammit.

Sky Sports launched Minus 1, a social content series focused on the Barclays Women's Super League. The format pairs professional footballers with personalities from music, culture and sport.

Technology & AI

Sky and BBC News received honours at the RTS Technology Awards 2026. Sky won recognition for its live HDR and Sky Glass technology, while BBC News won for its Sofie automation system.

Entries opened for the IBC2027 Accelerator Media Innovation Programme. Nine selected projects will present their concepts at the Kickstart Day hosted at the BBC Radio Theatre in London.

Radio & audio

Classic FM announced the return of its Pet Classics strand for 2026. The station will expand the broadcast across three evenings, offering five hours of calming music each night.

BBC local news executive Kate Squire is leaving the corporation after almost 42 years leading northern news teams. Long serving presenter Susie Fowler-Watt is also departing the BBC after 35 years.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Media mogul John Malone stated that the Trump administration informed Netflix that an acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery would face regulatory blocks. The decision prompted Netflix to withdraw, clearing the path for Paramount to buy the company.

In Canada, Rogers and Corus reached a deal to settle a carriage dispute that began in 2024. Rogers noted that CRTC fee rules had a significant impact on its quarterly revenue per user.

Results & money

Comcast reported a strong quarter for NBCUniversal and its Peacock streaming service. Peacock delivered a pre-tax revenue profit of $261 million, marking its second consecutive profitable quarter.

However, Comcast continues to face challenges in its cable and media empire. Declines in broadband and video subscribers weigh down overall performance despite boosts from sporting events and box-office releases.

Regulation & policy

Tech firms significantly increased spending on federal lobbying during the third quarter. Nvidia led AI lobbying expenditures with $2.2 million, followed by Anthropic at $2.1 million and OpenAI at $1.5 million.

News publishers are pushing back against Meta's updated terms of service, which take effect on 30 October. The News/Media Alliance called on CEO Mark Zuckerberg to halt changes that permit AI scraping of news content.

Audience & ratings

CBS recorded strong multiplatform ratings for its premiere week starting 4 October. The network averaged 8.63 million viewers, representing a 14% increase compared to its 2025 premiere week.

ABC broadcast affiliates expressed frustration over Disney's multiplatform Super Bowl plans. Affiliates warned that streaming on platforms like Disney+ and ESPN2 could reduce broadcast advertising revenue by up to 35%.

Technology & AI

CBC/Radio-Canada partnered with Dalet to modernise its newsrooms across Canada. The broadcaster will implement Dalet Pyramid to support digital-first news operations.

Roku released its Pro Series LX OLED TVs on Amazon in 55-inch and 65-inch models. Meanwhile, Samsung began selling certified renewed smartphones on Amazon with a one-year warranty.
