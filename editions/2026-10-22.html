United Kingdom
Deals & business

UKTV is expanding its portfolio with eight new FAST channels dedicated to BBC brands including Top Gear and EastEnders. These channels will be available on LG Channels and Freely.

Channel 4 has reached a deal with Warner Bros to bring the sitcom Friends back to the network in January 2027. Additionally, wedotv has launched its movie channel on the Freely platform with a refreshed slate of films.

Regulation & policy

The BBC has confirmed the closure of BBC Three, BBC Scotland and Radio 4 Extra as linear broadcast services. The corporation needs to make 500 million pounds in savings by 2028, leading to criticism from unions like Bectu regarding the impact on commissioning.

Ofcom is currently investigating a complaint from Huw Edwards concerning a Channel 5 drama. Meanwhile, MPs are scheduled to question Ofcom leadership on broadcasting impartiality and the impact of AI on the sector.

Audience & ratings

ITV has announced that its Big Brother brand has reached 202 million streams on ITVX. The current season has seen a significant increase in engagement compared to previous performance.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Disney+ and Hulu have greenlit a reboot of Glee, giving a series order to the Ryan Murphy musical dramedy. The decision comes after co-creators Ryan Murphy, Ian Brennan and Brad Falchuk finalized a new script. Disney streamer platforms are also expanding international content with a six-part Japanese horror anthology titled Night Tales Club.

Boat Rocker secured a co-exclusive streaming deal with Peacock and HBO Max for all five seasons of Orphan Black. In Canada, Rogers and Corus Entertainment settled their long-running distribution dispute. Meanwhile, Versant sold streaming rights for E! reality series The Golden Life to Hulu.

Audience & ratings

Netflix reduced its expected full-year ad revenue forecast from 3 billion dollars down to 2.7 billion dollars. The downward revision follows a slower rollout of its advertising capabilities. The platform is currently reassessing its broader content expenditure and global strategy.

CNN updated its digital presence with a redesigned homepage built on user insights to highlight video content. In cable news, Fox News maintained its position as the most-watched news network despite overall viewer gains at CNN.

Technology & AI

Amazon and Google introduced new software tools to lure developers away from Nvidia GPUs. Amazon launched TorchNeuron to help developers run PyTorch AI models on alternative chips. Google released a similar developer tool aiming to break Nvidia's stronghold on AI app development.

Anthropic announced Claude Duet, an AI agent designed to compete against Meta Muse and OpenAI Dots. The company also signed a lease for a 10-billion-dollar data centre in Nova Scotia to power its models by late 2027. Separately, Spotify launched a native in-car app for Android Automotive OS, beginning with select Audi vehicles.

Radio & audio

The Media Rating Council revoked Nielsen PPM accreditation in four major radio markets, including Philadelphia, Baltimore, Jacksonville and Pittsburgh. The council also denied accreditation for 18 additional markets, creating further uncertainty for radio audience measurement currencies.

Canadian broadcast regulators approved two radio transactions. The CRTC sanctioned the sale of Evanov's Lite 106.7 FM to Torres Media. The regulator also approved Hope Channel's acquisition of Christian radio stations in New Brunswick.
