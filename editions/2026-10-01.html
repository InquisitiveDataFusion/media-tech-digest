United Kingdom
Deals & business

BBC Studios is expanding its scripted production footprint with two new dedicated labels. Landed Productions will be based in Bristol to focus on drama development, while a second new label led by Deborah Sathe will focus on crime, mystery and thriller stories for UK and international markets.

The commercial arm of the BBC is also expanding its international reach. It has established a local production base and a dedicated development team in the Benelux region to drive the next phase of its global growth.

Meanwhile, Channel 4 chief executive Priya Dogra has dismissed any possibility of a merger with the BBC. Dogra stated that such a move would not be a merger of equals but would effectively amount to a BBC takeover.

Regulation & policy

Media regulator Ofcom has intervened in the broadband market by ordering Openreach to withdraw its wholesale fibre discount offer. The regulator ruled that the Incremental New to Openreach deal was unfair and could harm competition among network builders.

A parliamentary committee has also put forward proposals for the upcoming BBC Charter Review. MPs recommended that the public broadcaster must explore new revenue streams and spend more wisely when competing for programming.

Audience & ratings

The second season of BBC detective drama Ludwig has secured the biggest scripted launch of the year. The first episode attracted 9.1 million viewers over a 28-day period across television and streaming.

ITV is also seeing strong digital engagement with the latest series of Big Brother. The reality show has accumulated 12.7 million streams on ITVX, representing a 50 per cent increase on the same period last year.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

U.S. District Judge Araceli Martinez Olguin has approved the settlement between Paramount and 12 state attorneys general. This decision removes the final legal barrier to the 110 billion dollar acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery. Paramount is pushing a 44 billion dollar debt offering to finance the takeover.

The merger has faced criticism from Senator Corey Booker and actor Mark Ruffalo. Ruffalo argued the deal will stifle creativity and weaken free speech. Paramount legal chief Makan Delrahim dismissed concerns regarding the future independence of CNN under the new ownership.

Regulation & policy

The FTC is using its consumer protection powers to examine AI labs OpenAI and Anthropic. Investigators want to know if these companies have been transparent about the risks of their technology. This follows growing federal scrutiny of the artificial intelligence sector.

Senator Ron Wyden has raised concerns about the legality of an all Republican FCC panel. He asked Commissioner Anna Gomez for a legal opinion on whether the agency can operate without Democratic representation. This comes as speculation grows regarding potential leadership changes at the regulator.

Technology & AI

OpenAI is developing features to turn ChatGPT into a platform where users can discover and use software directly. This move aims to challenge the traditional app store model. The company is also working privately with Nvidia on agent safety despite being absent from a public industry effort.

Amazon Ads used its unBoxed conference to introduce new programmatic capabilities. These tools use AI to help advertisers manage full funnel campaigns with more transparency. Meanwhile, Acast has partnered with Roku to offer cross platform podcast advertising to Canadian viewers.
