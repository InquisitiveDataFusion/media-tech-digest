United Kingdom
Deals & business

Channel 4 Sales has won a major commercial partnership to represent Paramount's UK portfolio from 2027. The deal, valued at over £300 million, brings advertising for Channel 4 and Channel 5 under one roof for the first time.

Sky Sports has secured a three-year extension to its exclusive rights for the US Open. The agreement ensures the tennis tournament remains on the broadcaster's channels in the UK and Ireland until 2030.

Audience & ratings

Ofcom's News Report 2026 reveals that 21 per cent of UK adults now use generative artificial intelligence to access news. Despite this shift, traditional broadcast news continues to cut through in a highly fragmented media landscape.

ITV reports that its reality TV portfolio, including Love Island and Big Brother, has generated over 450 million streams this year. The genre reaches more than eight million viewers monthly, making it the second-largest on ITVX.

Radio & audio

Voice assistants like Alexa, Siri and Google Assistant could face Ofcom enforcement under new rules designed to protect access to internet radio. Meanwhile, the regulator has concluded that the future of radio includes issuing more FM and AM licences.

Industry responses to a recent consultation have urged the BBC to share its national DAB+ capacity. Respondents argued that small-scale DAB needs more freedom, stronger coverage and quicker decisions from Ofcom.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Paramount has informed California Attorney General Rob Bonta and Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass of plans to leave the state. The move comes as state attorneys general fight the company's proposed 111 billion dollar merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. Paramount and Bonta have finalised dates for settlement talks ahead of an antitrust trial scheduled for March.

Amazon Prime Video is launching its first five-service streaming bundle. The package includes Starz, AMC+, BritBox, MGM+ and PBS Masterpiece for 29.99 dollars per month. This combined offering represents a saving of nearly 40 percent compared to subscribing to each service individually.

Regulation & policy

Amazon, Netflix and YouTube have established the Streaming Access and Choice Alliance to lobby in Washington. Led by tech trade group TechNet, the alliance aims to promote high-value entertainment experiences amid growing backlash over the cost of sports on streaming platforms.

In Canada, telecom giants Rogers and Telus are asking the courts to decide whether the CRTC is being unfair in its crackdown on fees. Meanwhile, the Television Bureau has urged the FCC to overturn expanded eligibility for political advertising discounts following a Supreme Court emergency stay.

Audience & ratings

Apple TV broke the long-standing Emmy dominance of HBO and Netflix by taking home 28 trophies. The comedy series Widow's Bay led the sweep with 14 total awards. However, the overall Emmy Awards telecast on NBC saw early ratings dip by about 10 percent compared to the 2025 show.

The refurbished season debut of CBS newsmagazine 60 Minutes suffered a 19 percent ratings drop. The audience fell by 33 percent in the key 25 to 54 demographic, which may be linked to a weak NFL game lead-in. Conversely, Netflix drew 18.5 million US viewers for its live broadcast of the NFL game in Melbourne.

Technology & AI

OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind have engaged in weeks of discussions regarding AI safety. The labs are considering embedding independent safety evaluators inside their facilities. However, researchers warn that meaningful oversight will require true transparency and eventual regulation.

OpenAI has also acquired smartphone camera developer Glass Imaging for an estimated 300 million dollars. In Canada, Village Media has partnered with OpenAI to launch an AI-powered local information navigator called Open Door. Bell Canada is also pivoting its core business towards AI and sovereign data centres.
