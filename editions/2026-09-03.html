United Kingdom
Audience & ratings

Disney+ was the standout performer in the UK subscription video-on-demand market during the second quarter of 2026. The service added over 1.1 million UK households, largely driven by its integration into several Sky subscription packages.

Despite this growth, the wider UK streaming market showed signs of plateauing. Barb data revealed that 20.7 million UK homes had access to at least one subscription service in the second quarter, down slightly from 20.8 million in the previous quarter.

Technology & AI

Mobile connectivity in the UK is showing significant progress, particularly in high-speed infrastructure. The UK ranked second among the top ten European nations for its share of standalone 5G tests between January and July 2026.

This infrastructure boost is evident on the London Underground, where a long-running mobile blackspot is finally being resolved. A 20-year project is bringing continuous cellular and 5G coverage to passengers travelling through the subway network.

Radio & audio

BBC Radio 4 has announced the return of its surreal audio sketch show The Skewer for its seventeenth series. The programme will continue to blend news and satire using open submissions from the public.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Publicis Groupe has won the global media account for PepsiCo. Following this win, the agency group withdrew from the ongoing global media pitch for rival Coca-Cola.

A pro-merger group called Neighbors for Strong Communities is using artificial intelligence to pressure California Attorney General Rob Bonta. The group is generating AI letters urging Bonta to drop his antitrust lawsuit against the Paramount and Warner Bros. merger.

Actor Tom Cruise publicly supported the proposed Paramount and Warner Bros. merger. Cruise expressed confidence that the combined company will deliver on its promise to release 30 films a year.

Regulation & policy

President Donald Trump has threatened to report Meet the Press moderator Kristen Welker to the FCC. Trump objected to her comments stating that his endorsed political candidates have achieved mixed results. NBC News defended Welker, calling her one of the best in the business.

Former FCC Chairman Tom Wheeler and other media commentators criticised the threat as an attack on free speech and reality. They argued that the president is attempting to weaponise the regulatory agency against an individual journalist.

In another regulatory development, a federal court ruled that Google will not be forced to divest its adtech business. Although the court found Google operated illegal monopolies in publisher ad servers and ad exchanges, it rejected the Department of Justice proposal for a breakup.

Technology & AI

Apple Maps and Google Maps have renamed Lake Ontario to Lake America. The change follows an executive order signed by President Trump during a trade dispute with Canada. The Canadian version of Apple Maps still displays the original name.

Apple has asked a court for expedited discovery in its legal battle against OpenAI. Apple alleges that OpenAI is actively destroying evidence, pointing to a recently returned laptop used by a former employee accused of stealing data.

Roku has launched its first OLED television under the Roku Pro Series banner. The launch introduces new midrange competition against established models from LG, Samsung and Sony.

Radio & audio

AM and FM broadcasters face higher regulatory fees after the FCC rejected relief bids from the National Association of Broadcasters. The fee increases follow a rise in the regulatory agency's overall operating budget.

The FCC revoked the license of Montana FM station KBOQ in Lima. The station owner, Southwest Montana Media, failed to pay more than 6,700 dollars in delinquent regulatory fees.

Triton Digital has partnered with broadcast software provider RCS to enable programmatic advertising on over-the-air radio. The deal allows broadcasters to sell traditional radio ad inventory using existing streaming audio workflows.
