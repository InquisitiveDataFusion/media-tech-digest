United Kingdom
Results & money

BBC Deputy Director-General Rhodri Talfan Davies gave an update on the corporation's financial targets at a Broadcasting Press Guild event. The pubcaster has surpassed £200m in savings as it works towards a £500m target by 2028.

Regulation & policy

Culture Secretary Lisa Nandy has formally intervened in Sky's planned acquisition of ITV's Media & Entertainment business. She issued a Public Interest Intervention Notice to trigger investigations by UK watchdogs.

Ofcom will examine public interest concerns and apply a streaming test to the deal. The Competition and Markets Authority will review the transaction from a competition perspective.

Technology & AI

Sky News has expanded its strategic partnership with Arc XP as part of its 'Sky News 2030' initiative. Arc XP will supply technology for a redesigned app and website targeted to launch by the end of 2027.

ITV has renewed its partnership with video cybersecurity firm Friend MTS for two years. The anti-piracy vendor will continue providing 24/7 defence across ITV's linear channels and streaming platforms.

Radio & audio

Bauer and the IPA published a joint study examining audio campaign effectiveness. The research shows that combining audio with outdoor and publishing platforms delivers stronger brand trust and commercial outcomes.

Global united LBC and podcast studio team The News Agents for overnight coverage of the US midterm elections. Presenters reported live from the US throughout the night.

United States & Canada
Results & money

Roku delivered strong financial results for the third quarter of 2026, generating $1.5 billion in total revenue. The streaming provider earned $183 million in net income, easily beating Wall Street expectations due to robust subscription growth and higher media ad spending.

Sinclair Broadcast Group also posted notable growth, with total revenue rising 16 percent to $893 million. A 55 percent surge in political ad spend and strong live sports viewership helped the company top its full-year guidance.

Regulation & policy

The US Senate Commerce Committee rescheduled a hearing for Danielle Thumann Severs' nomination to the FCC. If confirmed, she will fill a Republican seat and establish a 3-1 conservative majority on the commission.

In Canada, the CRTC will hold a virtual hearing regarding an application for a new radio licence in Glencoe, Ontario. The regulator also approved an application by U Multicultural to add an Indigenous subchannel to its station in Winnipeg.

Technology & AI

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Microsoft announced support for a common robots.txt signal. This initiative aims to let publishers opt out of AI scraping through a single standard rather than managing each crawler individually.

OpenAI revealed that ChatGPT search queries have reached 20 percent of Google's search volume. Meanwhile, Google cut ties with set-top box maker Videostrong Technology after discovering a backdoor that turned 1.3 million devices into a global botnet.
