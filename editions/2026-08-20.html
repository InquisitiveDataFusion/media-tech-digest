United Kingdom
Deals & business

UK commercial broadcaster ITV has acquired the rights to stream all five seasons of the BBC period drama Poldark on ITVX. The series will become available on the streaming platform from 10 September. ITVX has also picked up the Latin American preschool animation series Doggyworld for a September debut.

UKTV has ordered a two-part documentary titled Surviving Fred and Rose West: Inside Cromwell Street for its streaming service U. The broadcaster has also renewed its detective drama Bookish for a Christmas special and a third season.

BBC Studios is expanding its presence in Germany by launching its BBC Earth and BBC Lifestyle FAST channels on the streaming service Joyn. Meanwhile, Disney-owned Hulu has acquired the crime documentary series The Promise from Banijay-owned UK production company Workerbee.

Audience & ratings

Sky Sports has agreed a strategic partnership with the podcast series The Fast and the Curious. The collaboration aims to reach more female and under-35 fans of Formula 1 through joint social media posts and content.

A new forecast by 3Vision projects that Western European streaming revenues will rise from 53.3 billion dollars in 2026 to 74.9 billion dollars by 2031. The growth will be driven by ad-supported and hybrid tiers outpacing traditional subscription growth.

Technology & AI

Sky is launching its Your Multiview service on 21 August to coincide with the return of the Premier League. The feature allows viewers to watch up to four live Sky Sports events at the same time on a single screen.

The service is available exclusively to Sky customers during busy periods of the sporting calendar.

Radio & audio

BBC Radio Scotland is launching a new monthly live music strand on 26 August. The series will feature live performances and interviews, beginning with the Scottish rock band Twin Atlantic.

BBC Radio 1 is bringing its New Music Live event back to Cardiff. The showcase will feature four emerging musical acts and will broadcast on the station on 30 September.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

The proposed 111 billion dollar merger between Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery has gained backing from major cinema chains AMC, Regal, and Cinemark. The exhibitors are urging California and other plaintiffs to settle their antitrust concerns to avoid market uncertainty. However, Los Angeles County projects the merger could threaten 2.78 billion dollars in local economic value, including substantial lost wages.

To mitigate delays, Paramount Skydance has asked a judge to force the twelve states and the Writers Guild of America to post a 1.88 billion dollar bond. This bond would cover financial losses incurred while the merger remains on hold pending trial. Meanwhile, CNN has secured its prime-time lineup ahead of the deal by signing a new contract with anchor Abby Phillip.

Regulation & policy

Disney and ABC have filed a lawsuit against the FCC and its chair, Brendan Carr. The network claims the agency is conducting a retaliatory campaign that violates First Amendment protections by targeting its broadcast licenses. The legal battle began after the FCC ordered ABC-owned stations to submit early license renewals following challenges to programming on The View.

Industry experts suggest the network has a strong legal case, though appeals could delay a final resolution. The outcome of the lawsuit could set a significant precedent for how the FCC reviews programming under its public interest standard. Meanwhile, internal documents have revealed close ties between the FCC chair, the White House, and right-wing media outlets.

Audience & ratings

Nielsen is overhauling its TV ratings methodology ahead of the new television season. The measurement firm will rely more heavily on data from partner wearable devices to capture co-viewing and demographic details. The changes aim to provide more accurate ratings for media and advertising clients.

The final traditional edition of Nielsen's Gauge report showed that streaming has reached a record 48.5 percent share of total TV viewing. In the streaming market, Peacock has raised its subscription prices again after achieving its first profitable quarter. To retain users, Peacock is also testing a new membership rewards program featuring merchandise discounts and free pizzas.
