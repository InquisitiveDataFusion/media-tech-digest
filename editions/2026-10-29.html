United Kingdom
Deals & business

Channel 4 and The Walt Disney Company have struck an agreement to bring Channel 4's linear HD channel and on-demand programmes to Disney+ in the UK and Ireland. The partnership will see multiple Channel 4 shows launch on the streaming platform from early 2027.

In distribution acquisitions, ITV has bought local 1990s crime drama The Chief to stream on ITVX. Meanwhile, UKTV plans to move its London headquarters from Hammersmith to Television Centre in White City by spring 2027 to sit closer to BBC Studios teams.

Regulation & policy

Media regulator Ofcom has outlined methods for social media platforms to verify user ages. Recommended checks include photo ID, digital ID, banking checks and facial age estimation technology.

Separately, the European Broadcasting Union called on Meta to revise commercial terms that permit AI tools to scrape publisher websites. Ofcom also warned that the shift to streaming could widen Northern Ireland sports blackouts due to technological and regulatory limits.

Technology & AI

Virgin Media O2 has extended its Gig2 broadband service to its hybrid fibre-coaxial network. The upgrade brings 2Gbps speeds beyond its existing full-fibre footprint to cover roughly 16 million premises.

Meta has expanded its Instagram video experience by launching a dedicated app on Apple TV. Elsewhere, Netflix released an updated mobile app tailored for Apple's newly released foldable iPhone Duo.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Disney Plus has signed a first of its kind collaboration with Channel 4 to bring local content to the streamer. The deal covers distinctive programming that will launch on Disney Plus in the UK and Ireland in early 2027.

Amazon has received FCC clearance to acquire Globalstar for 11.5 billion dollars. This move strengthens Amazon's position in the mobile-satellite sector through its Leo division.

Results & money

Microsoft reported 10 billion dollars in revenue from its partnership with OpenAI for the quarter ending 30 September. This figure highlights the massive financial dependency between the two companies.

YouTube ad revenue growth slowed to 9 percent this quarter. While the platform reached 11.2 billion dollars in sales, it missed overall earnings estimates as Google Cloud outperformed the video division.

Netflix has lowered its advertising revenue projections to 2.7 billion dollars. The company is facing a glut of streaming commercials and a squeeze on digital ad spending.

Audience & ratings

Traditional broadcast television watch time fell to 17.5 percent in August 2026. This is a significant drop from the 25 percent recorded by Nielsen in May 2021.

Fox News is on track for its lowest rated month since late 2023. Despite a 32 percent drop in the key advertiser demographic, it remains the most watched cable news network.

Technology & AI

Amazon has launched the Echo Show 16 to compete with Apple's smaller smart home screens. The new device is more TV oriented and carries a higher price point than rival products.

Apple's latest Safari tracking restrictions are pushing the ad tech industry toward server side solutions. Industry parties are rallying behind the Trusted Server Framework to manage these changes.
