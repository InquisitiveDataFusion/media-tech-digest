United Kingdom
Deals & business

Sky and ITV are exploring content sharing for cricket rights to broaden the sport's appeal. The ECB wants more free to air coverage in the next contract cycle starting in 2029.

Tubi has expanded its UK catalogue through deals with NBCUniversal and Sony Pictures. The ad supported service added 450 movies and television episodes to its local offering.

Disney+ has secured a creator led football deal and a 20 episode series with Dhar Mann Studios. These moves reflect a strategy to engage audiences through established digital creators.

Results & money

Netflix has reshaped the British television landscape by surpassing 2 billion pounds in UK revenue. This financial milestone places the global streamer ahead of ITV for the first time.

The shift comes as streaming giants face a ceiling on subscription costs. Research shows that price increases in Western Europe have been among the highest in the world recently.

Audience & ratings

Experts at the Edinburgh TV Festival cautioned the BBC against using traditional TV methods for YouTube. They suggest the broadcaster must adapt its commissioning style to reach younger viewers effectively.

Streaming platforms are also donating advertising inventory to the ASA public awareness campaign. Netflix and Waitrose are the latest brands to support the initiative which has run since 2022.

BBC Music confirmed it will provide live coverage of the Reading and Leeds Festival 2026. Special shows will air across Radio 1 and 6 Music during the event.

Absolute Radio presenter Dan Noble is launching a new comedy podcast focused on a zombie apocalypse. The series features guest comedians and debuts at the start of September.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

California Attorney General Rob Bonta called off settlement talks with Paramount regarding its proposed 111 billion dollar acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery. Bonta accused Paramount of acting in bad faith and leaking details of the discussions. Meanwhile, Republican attorneys general from Iowa and Montana asked the Supreme Court to block the antitrust lawsuit led by California.

Nexstar Media Group selected a new board of directors for its Tegna subsidiary. A federal judge had ruled that the previous board, which consisted of Nexstar executives, violated a prior court order. Despite ongoing litigation, the combined Nexstar and Tegna entity remains the largest television station group in the United States.

Results & money

A report from the CRTC revealed that online services captured 40 percent of Canadian broadcasting revenues in the 2025 broadcast year. This milestone comes as traditional television and radio revenues continue to decline. Bell Media reported that subscriber growth for its Crave streaming service has allowed the platform to take bolder content risks.

Research from Ampere Analysis showed that the rate of subscription price increases for major streaming services has slowed. Average price hikes for Netflix, Disney Plus, and Amazon fell from 24 percent in 2023-24 to 14 percent in 2025-26.

Regulation & policy

A federal appeals court set aside an FCC Media Bureau notice that would have allowed political parties to receive the lowest unit charge for broadcast campaign ads. The court sided with four Democratic candidates who challenged the guidance. FCC Commissioner Anna Gomez had previously warned that the bureau's interpretation would unleash dark money into the upcoming midterms.

The CRTC announced it will no longer require most Canadian radio stations to undergo regular, fixed-term licence renewals. Under the new policy, the regulator granted indefinite broadcasting licences to hundreds of AM and FM stations to reduce administrative burdens.

Media watchdog groups filed a motion to intervene in Disney's First Amendment lawsuit against the FCC. The legal battle began after Disney sued the agency over investigations into diversity, equity, and inclusion policies at its ABC stations.

Technology & AI

Amazon will add two million Nvidia graphics processing chips to its data centres over the next two years. The company tripled its order to keep pace with surging demand for cloud and artificial intelligence services. Nvidia expects its quarterly revenue to reach 108 billion dollars in the coming months.
