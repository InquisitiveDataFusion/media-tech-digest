United Kingdom
Deals & business

ITV has commissioned a 12-part linear television series based on a popular YouTube format. The show, produced by Middlechild Production, follows gardener Alan Titchmarsh as he shares his horticultural experience.

Comcast Technology Solutions has secured a multi-year agreement with A+E Global Media. The technology provider will manage the distribution of premium on-demand content across major streaming and pay-television platforms.

Results & money

Channel 4 is cutting 340 jobs, which represents 28 per cent of its workforce, by the end of the year. The broadcaster described the layoffs as part of a long-term transformation strategy to create a leaner operating model.

Trade body Bectu called the decision deeply worrying for a television industry already facing a severe commission slump. The cuts aim to redirect more revenues towards distinctive British content.

Meanwhile, Scotland's STV Group reported that its revenues fell by 27 per cent to 66.1 million pounds. The company blamed a slump in commissions and a strategic pivot to digital content for the decline.

Technology & AI

BBC Studios and its subsidiary UKTV have signed a multi-year agreement with German provider Full Season. The media services company will handle playout and distribution for 53 linear channels and associated on-demand services.

Sky has upgraded its Sky Go application for Sky Glass and Sky Stream customers. The redesigned app brings more of the Sky OS experience to mobile devices and tablets, allowing users to resume viewing easily.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Paramount and Skydance have reiterated their demand that twelve states and the Writers Guild of America post a $1.88 billion bond. This security would cover losses if their $110 billion merger with Warner Bros. Discovery is delayed past the trial. Courts have historically resisted issuing such massive bonds in antitrust merger cases.

Meanwhile, the US Department of Justice is intensifying its scrutiny of other major media transactions. Both Fox and Roku have received federal requests for additional information and documentary material regarding their proposed $22 billion deal. Fox stated that the regulatory review and document requests were expected.

Regulation & policy

Public television stations and PBS have expressed support for the Federal Communications Commission's efforts to modernise the Emergency Alert System. They back a proposal to permit software-based alert technologies. Separately, the FCC is preparing to vote on opening up more than 1,000 MHz of spectrum for satellite and internet-of-things services.

A defamation lawsuit filed by California Governor Gavin Newsom against Fox News has been scheduled for trial. The governor is seeking $787 million in punitive damages. The trial is set to begin in March 2028.

Technology & AI

Apple has introduced its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, featuring a 7.6-inch screen when opened. The device is priced at $1,999 and includes new artificial intelligence features. Apple also launched a new image provenance tool to help users verify whether photos were captured on an iPhone or generated by AI.

Netflix is working as a pre-release partner to adapt its streaming application for the new foldable screen. Other hardware manufacturers are also active, with Samsung offering semiconductor supply chain support to OpenAI. Huawei and Xiaomi have also progressed their own foldable and trifold designs to compete with Samsung.
