United Kingdom
Deals & business

Disney+ has agreed a one-year sponsorship and content deal with soccer podcast producer The Overlap. The multimillion-pound package includes Stick to Football and a new format, Stick to United, featuring Wayne Rooney. This move serves as a direct rival to Netflix's partnership with Goalhanger.

Sky is preparing the return of Saturday Night Live UK. The broadcaster has ordered six more episodes of the comedy show, retaining all 11 cast members from its debut season.

Channel 4 has acquired the Australian medical comedy-drama The F Ward. Distributed by Paramount Global Content Distribution, the series has been licensed to 100 international markets, making it the fastest-selling Australian drama pre-launch.

Results & money

The UK fixed broadband market has entered a flat phase. Total retail and wholesale connections reached an estimated 29.37 million at the end of the second quarter of 2026. This represents a rise of just 1,000 connections quarter-on-quarter.

Consumer segments accounted for the vast majority of these connections, making up approximately 27.06 million of the total UK broadband market.

Technology & AI

UK free-to-air broadcaster Narrative Entertainment has partnered with Nagravision. The broadcaster will use the OpenTV ENTera platform to improve personalisation, audience measurement and service development across its connected TV and streaming operations.

Virgin Media O2 has expanded its IPTV offering by adding the Tennis Channel. The 24-hour service is available on channel 542 for Virgin TV 360 and Flex customers at no extra cost.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Paramount has obtained regulatory clearance from Mexico for its merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. The transaction has now satisfied conditions in 68 countries. A lawsuit by 12 US state attorneys general remains the final obstacle to closing the deal.

The legal battle has created divisions within Hollywood. The Directors Guild of America and IATSE are seeking a settlement to keep production in the US. However, the Writers Guild of America is actively fighting to block the merger.

Amid the regulatory delays, Warner Bros. Discovery chief executive David Zaslav has sold over 195 million dollars in company stock. Meanwhile, Paramount has rejected reports that it is willing to sell CNN to secure a settlement.

Regulation & policy

Disney chief executive Josh D'Amaro has asserted that ABC will maintain its journalistic independence. The broadcaster is currently involved in a dispute with the Federal Communications Commission. D'Amaro stated that the network will not be told how to run its business.

Paramount is also considering moving CBS News out of New York City. This potential relocation comes as legal tensions rise with attorneys general in New York and California. Chief executive David Ellison has also threatened to move Paramount out of California if the antitrust suit is not settled.

Technology & AI

A US federal judge has ordered Google to make it easier for users to install alternative app stores. Google has been given one week to implement the changes on its Google Play platform. The ruling follows a long-running legal dispute with Epic Games.

Apple has proposed a new fee structure for purchases made outside its App Store. The company wants to collect a commission of up to 15 percent on external transactions. Epic Games has opposed the request, calling it unreasonable.

In other technology developments, Apple is negotiating with publishers to pay for content to improve its Siri voice assistant. The deals aim to provide the artificial intelligence tool with current news. Separately, Anthropic is discussing a six billion dollar acquisition of Israeli startup Decart.
