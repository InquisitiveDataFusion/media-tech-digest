Deals & business

Paramount reached a settlement with 12 state attorneys general and the WGA to clear its $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery. The deal includes commitments to maintain studio lots and invest $300 million annually in US film production.

David Ellison aims to close the merger in approximately two weeks. The combined entity faces significant challenges, including a debt load exceeding $77 billion. Reports suggest Paramount may seek equity investment from Elon Musk to bolster its financial position.

Regulation & policy

Major news organisations, including CBS, Fox News, and NPR, filed for a restraining order to end a White House press ban. The legal action follows the exclusion of CNN, MS NOW, and Politico from the press pool.

NPR leadership condemned the bans as a direct assault on press freedom. The industry remains focused on the legal battle to restore access for all journalists.
