United Kingdom
Deals & business

Samsung TV Plus and BBC Studios have agreed to launch four channels across 11 European markets. These include BBC Lifestyle and a dedicated EastEnders channel for the Benelux region. The deal expands the presence of British public service content on free ad-supported streaming platforms.

ITV Studios has secured global distribution rights for Incognito, a new social experiment format from Derren Brown. The show was originally produced for Channel 4. Meanwhile, the BBC has made its first acquisition of a Brazilian Globoplay original with the fantasy series Blood Red.

The BBC has renewed the legal drama The Split Up for a second season. It has also renewed the school drama Waterloo Road. These commissions come as the broadcaster seeks new entertainment formats for its primetime Saturday slots.

Regulation & policy

The BBC is seeking a court order to force Donald Trump to sit for a deposition. This relates to a 10 billion dollar defamation lawsuit filed by the former president. Both parties had previously agreed to a date in October before legal delays occurred.

BBC content chief Kate Phillips has criticised the use of algorithms in commissioning. She argued that relying on data rather than human instinct creates uninspired programming. Phillips described algorithmically driven content as a creative carcass.

Technology & AI

CNN is launching its All Access subscription service in the UK on 20 October. This marks the first international expansion for the platform. It will offer a centralised destination for live and on-demand journalism.

Roku research suggests that interactive and home screen advertisements have a strong future in the UK market. Media planners agree that these formats are becoming more popular with consumers. Channel 4 is also experimenting with digital formats by launching its first branded microdrama.

Radio & audio

A study of sports news bulletins shows that football accounts for over 74 percent of talkSPORT coverage. This compares to roughly 30 percent on BBC Radio 5 Live. The data highlights the heavy reliance on men's football for commercial sports radio.

The BBC has cancelled plans for a new studio for The Archers in Birmingham. This decision may lead to the long running radio soap being recorded at a different location. The move follows wider cost cutting measures across the corporation.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Shareholders for both Fox Corp and Roku have voted to approve a 22 billion dollar merger. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027 following regulatory reviews.

Disney is seeking to acquire assets from Character.AI for 20 million dollars. This follows the hiring of the startup's former chief executive as Disney's chief technology officer.

Tegna and Disney renewed ABC affiliation agreements across 13 markets. The multi-year deal ensures ABC programming remains on Tegna stations in states including Texas, California and Florida.

Regulation & policy

The US Department of Justice has opened an antitrust investigation into five major news organisations. Investigators are looking into whether ABC News, CBS News, NBC News, CNN and Fox News violated the Sherman Act.

A federal judge has prohibited the Trump administration from banning CNN, MS NOW and Politico from White House grounds. The preliminary injunction ensures these outlets retain access for the foreseeable future.

ABC has filed court documents alleging the FCC used license renewals to retaliate against Disney. The network claims regulators bypassed standard options to punish the company over its programming choices.

Audience & ratings

CNN is expanding its All Access subscription service to Canada and the United Kingdom. The service includes live and on-demand programming with specific sponsorship opportunities for international events.

NBC is bringing its true crime series Dateline to Canada via Citytv. The local version is scheduled to launch in late November with correspondent Keith Morrison.

PBS reached a deal to provide France 24 streaming channels to its member stations. The agreement includes nine live, commercial-free English language channels for digital platforms.

Technology & AI

Apple has introduced HomeView, a new smart display priced at 299 dollars. The device is part of a broader push into artificial intelligence and home automation markets.

OpenAI has accused a former employee of taking over 100 files related to product plans before joining Apple. A federal judge ordered OpenAI to turn over hiring records and interview materials as part of the trade secrets case.

Microsoft announced its third generation Maia in-house AI chip to reduce reliance on Nvidia hardware. The chip is designed to handle cloud AI workloads and the latest reasoning models.
