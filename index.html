United Kingdom
Deals & business

Disney+ has agreed a one-year sponsorship and content deal with Gary Neville's sports platform, The Overlap. The streaming service will become the headline sponsor for Stick to Football and a new format called Stick to United. This move is seen as a direct response to Netflix's partnership with Goalhanger.

Warner Bros Discovery Access has commissioned UK production companies Project Must Entertainment, 7Fifty, and Double FTV. These producers will create culinary programming for the Food Network brand and the Discovery+ streaming platform.

Samsung TV Plus has expanded its partnership with distributor All3Media International. The deal adds nine new dedicated FAST channels to the platform's line-up in the UK, France, and Spain. Meanwhile, Virgin Media O2 has added the Tennis Channel to its streaming line-up for Virgin TV customers.

Regulation & policy

Media regulator Ofcom is investigating GB News following 12,000 complaints about a guest's remarks. The guest appeared to link pride parades with bestiality and paedophilia. The incident has set a potential record for complaints against the broadcaster.

Ofcom has also found Nation Radio London in breach of broadcasting rules. The station aired a prohibited political advertisement. The broadcast occurred despite the script receiving prior clearance from Radiocentre.

UK brands are adapting their media strategies to align with new regulations on HFSS in-store promotions. The restrictions on high fat, sugar, and salt products have doubled interest in omnichannel advertising. Brands are developing targeted campaigns to navigate the rules across different channels.

Audience & ratings

The UK fixed broadband market has entered a flat phase, according to data from Point Topic. Total retail and wholesale connections reached 29.37 million at the end of the second quarter of 2026. This represents an increase of just 1,000 connections quarter-on-quarter.

Journalist Ray Snoddy has highlighted issues with how The Times reported its own radio station's listener figures. The investigation suggests there was under-reporting of RAJAR data. This has raised questions about transparency in media reporting.

The Alliance of Media Research Analysts has expanded its annual awards by adding six new categories. These additions address the underrepresentation of regional and independent agencies. They also recognise the growing role of adtech in media research and honour the late judge Denise Turner.

Radio & audio

Lyca Radio is set to expand its Midlands network by launching full DAB coverage in Birmingham. The transition will take place on 1 September. This move will allow the station to reach a wider audience in the region.

Grampian Hospital Radio has been honoured with the King's Award for Voluntary Service. The award recognises decades of dedication by the station's volunteers. The team has provided entertainment and comfort to patients for many years.

UK video-first podcast company Jampot has partnered with Sony to upgrade its production capabilities. Jampot has deployed a range of Sony cameras and solutions to support its growing slate of visual podcasts. This setup aims to deliver cinematic quality for digital listeners.

Regulation & policy

The Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission is considering a major policy shift [10, 11]. The regulator may remove licence expiry dates and issue indefinite television broadcast licences [10, 11]. This proposal would affect major media companies including Bell Media, Rogers, and Corus [10, 11].

The CRTC has also administratively renewed several broadcasting licences to August 2027 [34]. These one-year extensions apply to broadcasters such as Bell Media, Rogers, Blue Ant, and Corus [18].
