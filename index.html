United Kingdom
Results & money

ITV reported a two per cent decline in group external revenue for the first nine months of 2026, reaching £2.76bn. Advertising demand weakened significantly in the third quarter, dropping eight per cent due to macroeconomic headwinds and shifting digital spend.

To offset the downturn, the commercial broadcaster will slash £30m from its programming budget. Despite linear advertising pressure, viewing on ITVX rose 29 per cent over the nine-month period.

Regulation & policy

Media regulator Ofcom has updated its guidance under Section Seven of the Broadcasting Code. The new rules require broadcasters to step up welfare measures for participants in constructed reality programmes.

The guidelines focus on formats involving emotional or physical intimacy. Broadcasters must ensure adequate risk assessments and support systems for contributors before, during and after production.

Audience & ratings

Barb reported that 21 million UK households had access to a subscription video-on-demand service in the third quarter of 2026. This represents 71 per cent of UK homes, up from 20.7 million in the previous quarter.

Separately, a poll revealed that 79 per cent of the public wants the government to guarantee Freeview services until at least 2044. Campaigners are calling on the Prime Minister to include this protection in the next BBC Charter.

Technology & AI

Sky has started rolling out a refreshed version of Sky OS to Sky Glass and Sky Stream subscribers across the UK. The update adds conversational voice search, updated navigation and clearer recording controls.

Ozone has launched OzoneOS to help publishers pass first-party audience insights into programmatic transactions. The platform aims to ensure premium publisher inventory is accurately valued by buyers.

Deals & business

Skydance has outlined major operational changes following its acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery. The company expects to achieve 6 billion dollars in synergies by eliminating management layers, combining operations and reducing technology spend.

The media group confirmed plans to launch a joint streaming bundle featuring HBO Max and Paramount Plus. Meanwhile, restructuring has already hit the interactive unit, with Skydance Games cutting at least 25 staff following the merger of Paramount Games Studio and Warner Bros Games.

Regulation & policy

New York state has enacted a comprehensive artificial intelligence safety law targeting major developers such as OpenAI and Anthropic. The legislation requires companies to register, outline safety protocols, and report critical safety incidents under penalty of financial fines.

FCC nominee Danielle Thumann Severs faced questioning regarding Chairman Brendan Carr's disputes with broadcast networks. Separately, Democratic Senator Richard Blumenthal demanded the release of FCC records concerning First Amendment investigations.
