United Kingdom
Deals & business

UKTV and Masterpiece have joined a new period crime drama titled Fleet Street from the creators of Miss Scarlet. The six part series will launch on the free streaming service U and the U&DRAMA channel.

Disney plus has greenlit a five part adaptation of Bob Mortimer's mystery novel The Satsuma Complex. The series will be produced by ITV Studios label Mainstreet Pictures as a Hulu Original.

Amazon Kids plus and ITV have co-commissioned a new children's competition format called Shrunk. The show features participants taking part in challenges within gigantic worlds.

Results & money

The Walt Disney Company reported strong fourth quarter results with revenue reaching 25.9 billion dollars. Streaming businesses delivered significant growth with operating income for the company growing by 43 per cent.

Disney intends to raise its 2027 content budget by 9 per cent to 26 billion dollars. A key focus of this investment will be tripling the output of international original programming for Disney plus.

Regulation & policy

Channel 4 has reinforced the position of UK public service broadcasters by calling for a managed switch off of digital terrestrial television in 2034. The broadcaster argues that extending Freeview beyond this date would be unsustainable.

This move is a formal response to the government Media Green Paper. The broadcasters are seeking a certain and sustainable future for television distribution as audiences migrate to internet based platforms.

Technology & AI

Sky is rolling out a free upgrade to Sky OS for its Glass and Stream customers. The update includes a refreshed homepage and a simplified top menu to improve navigation.

New features include conversational voice search and greater control over cloud recordings. This represents the most significant update to the platform since its initial launch.

United States & Canada
Deals & business

Quebecor has gone public with a non-binding 31 billion dollar proposal to acquire Telus Corporation. The deal aims to protect Canadian digital sovereignty by combining networks across the country. Telus directors have acknowledged the unsolicited offer but expressed initial concerns.

Following the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery into Skydance, the company is consolidating its production assets. Warner Bros. Television Group and Paramount Television Studios will merge into a single unit in early 2027. This move follows a strategic review of the combined company's television operations.

Skydance and Comcast have also decided to wind down their European streaming joint venture SkyShowtime in 2027. This follows the closing of the 110 billion dollar Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery merger. Netflix CEO Ted Sarandos noted that while Warner would have been a nice acquisition, the price was too high.

Results & money

Disney posted revenue of 25.95 billion dollars for its fiscal fourth quarter, beating Wall Street estimates. Growth was driven by streaming gains, advertising, and strong performance at its theme parks. The company is now exploring a free streaming product to complement its existing services.

Fubo reported a net loss of 51.8 million dollars for the last quarter. To add value for customers, Fubo is now offering the Disney Plus and Hulu ad-supported bundle for free to its Pro and Elite subscribers. This comes as Disney prepares to fully merge the Disney Plus and Hulu apps in December.

Regulation & policy

Newsmax is suing to block the integration of Fox and Roku, claiming the 22 billion dollar deal creates an unfair competitor. The lawsuit alleges the combined entity would control 71 percent of the American FAST market. Newsmax claims this dominance threatens the rapidly expanding market for free advertising-supported television.

Republicans are rushing to confirm Danielle Thumann Severs as the third Republican on the FCC before the new year. Democratic members boycotted the Senate Commerce Committee meeting in protest. The move aims to secure a Republican quorum on the commission before the change in government control.

Technology & AI

Microsoft has launched new AI coding models that it claims rival Claude 5.5 at a fraction of the cost. These models are intended to reduce reliance on external partners like OpenAI and Anthropic. Meanwhile, Amazon and Google are set to own nearly 30 percent of Anthropic following its initial public offering.

Apple faces criticism from iFixit over its new HomeView hub, which proved difficult to repair during a teardown. The company also dealt with widespread service outages affecting iCloud, the App Store, and Apple Music this week. Separately, California has sued OpenAI over alleged failures to implement appropriate AI safety safeguards.
